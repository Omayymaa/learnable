About LearnAble

At LearnAble, we believe that dyslexia is not a limitation—it's a different way of learning that deserves recognition and support. Founded by passionate educators and advocates, our NGO is dedicated to creating an inclusive learning environment where every dyslexic student can thrive.

We provide personalized educational support, assistive technology, specialized tutoring, and confidence-building programs tailored to each student's unique needs. Our holistic approach addresses not only academic challenges but also emotional and social development.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower dyslexic students with the tools, resources, and support they need to overcome learning barriers and achieve their full academic and personal potential.

How We Help

📖

Specialized Tutoring

One-on-one sessions with trained educators who understand dyslexia and use proven multi-sensory teaching methods.

💻

Assistive Technology

Access to text-to-speech software, audiobooks, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and other digital tools that make learning easier.

🎨

Creative Workshops

Art, music, and hands-on activities that build confidence and develop strengths beyond traditional academics.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Support

Guidance and resources for parents and caregivers to better understand and support their child's learning journey.

🤝

Peer Mentoring

Connecting students with successful dyslexic mentors who inspire and share coping strategies.

🏫

School Advocacy

Working with educational institutions to create more inclusive and understanding learning environments.
